The problem

Most GTM stacks are tools, not systems.

Leads, sequences and replies live in separate places. Things fall between them.

Lists go stale

Research done once and never refreshed ages quickly.

Messages sound generic

Copy that ignores the buyer's context gets ignored back.

Replies get lost

A good reply that waits two days is a cold reply.

Nobody owns it

Without a clear owner and documentation, the setup drifts.

How an engagement works

Six steps, no surprises.

  1. Intro call20 minutes to understand your product, buyers and current setup.
  2. One-page planA short written plan covering goal, audience, angles and systems.
  3. Short pilotA small, scoped first build to test the approach on real accounts.
  4. BuildWe set up the workflows and connect them to your tools.
  5. ReviewWe look at real replies together and decide what to keep.
  6. HandoffDocumentation and a walkthrough so your team can run it.

Pricing is quoted after a call. Timelines depend on scope and are agreed in the plan.
