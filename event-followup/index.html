Event follow-up

Don't let your badge scans go cold.

Events produce a pile of contacts and a short window to follow up. We build the flow that turns that pile into personal, timely messages.

Before

Plan the follow-up

During

Capture cleanly

After

Follow up fast

Draft personal messages, route replies and hand warm contacts to your team.

  • Your contact list or scan export
  • A short description of your product and buyers
